## Root cause of the 2,040 vs 2,878 difference (from Ebrahim's PDF "CAST AI Fees and Net Savings" section, verified arithmetically)

The report **mixed two metrics across clusters**: helios ~1,774 average *provisioned* + integ 258.5 *requested* + kronos 7.6 *requested* = **2,040.1** × €5 = €10,200.50/month. Requested vCPU is a workload-demand metric used in savings modeling; it is **not** the licence billing metric. The €10,200.50 fee line is understated and superseded.

## Why provisioned > requested, per cluster (subagent-verified, data-bound)

- **Helios:** provisioned exceeded requested on all 30 days (mean gap 436.7 vCPU ≈ 24.4%); node quantization (system/DaemonSet overhead, instance-sized steps 302→2,445 vCPU); requests padded (used = 12.25% of requested); RAM skew (RAM packing 48.5% vs CPU 75.5%).
- **Integ:** burst cluster — daily provisioned swung 114.9 → 1,988.9 vCPU (17.3×; peaks Sep 20/21/30); burst hours are fully time-weighted into the month mean; provisioned = 1.58× requested.
- **Kronos:** weekly demand cycle (weekday prov 91.1 vs weekend 74.1; 12.6× monthly range); min-node floor on a small fleet; RAM-driven packing (116.5 GiB requested vs 311.3 GiB provisioned).
- All provisioned capacity on all three clusters is on-demand; no spot anywhere (spot disabled by policy).
