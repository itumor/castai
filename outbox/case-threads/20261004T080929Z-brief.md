## Root cause of the 2,040 vs 2,878 difference (from Ebrahim's own PDF, "CAST AI Fees and Net Savings" section)

- Helios: **~1,774 average *provisioned* vCPUs** used as fee basis
- Integ: **258.5 *requested* vCPUs** used (from workload-summary data)
- Kronos: **7.6 *requested* vCPUs** used (from workload-summary data)
- 1,774 + 258.5 + 7.6 = 2,040.1 → × €5 = €10,200.50/month in the report

i.e. the report mixed two different metrics (provisioned for Helios, requested for Integ/Kronos). The €10,200.50/month estimate is therefore understated and superseded.

## Answer (verdict)

The fee basis is **average provisioned (node-capacity) vCPUs**, confirmed by the CAST AI billing API (feature=phase2). Lars' 2,878 vCPUs is the correct number for the trio's fee; 2,040 (requested) is not the billing metric. My report's fee estimate must be corrected.
