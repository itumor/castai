## Why this can happen

For agent-based (read-only) connections, the account is normally auto-discovered from the EC2 instance identity of the node the agent runs on — and that **cannot** produce a wrong account, because EKS worker nodes always live in the cluster's own account. A stored-but-wrong account therefore means it was **pinned as explicit input during onboarding** (e.g. the connect script/parameters were generated while pointed at account 488305652903), and CAST AI stored it unverified. What CAST AI stores is onboarding-time *input*, not a value it computes or rewrites — which is exactly why a mismatch like yours is possible in the first place.

That leaves two candidate root causes, both on the "what got connected" side rather than "CAST AI changed something":

- **(a) Caller-account mix-up at connect time** — the onboarding on 2026-07-31 ran with parameters pointing at 488305652903 (the account that hosts 9 of your 13 HAFAS clusters and was being bulk-onboarded that same day), while the actual `timemachine` cluster lives in 356676076018.
- **(b) Same-named twin clusters** — an EKS cluster named `timemachine` exists in *both* accounts, and CAST AI was connected to the one in 488305652903 while Wiz/CUR track the one in 356676076018. (Same-name clusters are common in large fleets — CAST AI identifies clusters by its own UUID, not by name.)
