## "Why don't they see the savings" — answer skeleton (evidence-labeled)

1. **Savings are counterfactual, not a falling bill** (API fact + verified math): CAST AI
   compares actual spend vs a frozen pre-CAST-AI baseline. Workload demand grew
   (helios avg vCPU 1,759 Sep → ~1,991 Oct-01), so the absolute AWS bill grows while
   the unit price falls (ER 0.86/0.70/0.62).
2. **They look at a different ruler** (verified): Siemens watches Cloudability/SAP in EUR,
   paid/RI-SP-discounted; CAST AI reports discounted-compute-basis USD counterfactual.
   Levels will never match; only deltas/ER are comparable until their rate card `d` is pinned.
3. **The platform fee consumes a visible chunk** (verified): fee ∝ avg provisioned vCPU
   (≈$5.78/vCPU·mo, i.e. the €5 contract rate) — on Helios it is ~half of gross savings;
   net column = gross − fee (validated numerically to <$10/cluster residual).
4. **Storage sits outside the savings math** (verified): monthly savings are compute-only;
   helios storage alone is ~$8.8k/mo and appears in the bill but never in savings.
5. **No spot, no workload rightsizing yet** (verified): spot spend = $0 org-wide, WOOP
   `optimizedCount: 0` on all three → all realized savings come from node downscaling/
   bin-packing vs the frozen baseline only.
