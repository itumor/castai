- name-independent identification of the app — an explicit reference (e.g. by flag, value, or annotation keyed on the client id) rather than matching on display name.

**In the meantime — your Terraform ask already works today.** Since Terraform is your recommended path: the `castai_aks_cluster` resource already takes a pre-created Entra Application straight from your self-service portal — nothing gets created by our tooling on this path:

- `client_id` — your pre-created Azure AD application ID (required)
- `client_secret` — the application password (optional, sensitive)
- `federation_id` — optional, for **secretless auth via workload-identity federation impersonation**; this may suit Siemens CI/CD even better, since no client secret is stored in the pipeline at all

So the Terraform/OpenTofu flow you described is usable as-is: the Entra App comes out of your internal self-service, you pass its client id (+ secret, or federation) into the resource, done. Reference (generated from the provider source):

- AKS onboarding overview: https://docs.cast.ai/docs/terraform-provider-aks
- Cloud permissions (roles the app needs): https://docs.cast.ai/docs/cloud-permissions
