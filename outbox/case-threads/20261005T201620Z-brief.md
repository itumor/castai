## Q2 grounding (Karpenter + CAST Workload Autoscaler) [docs]

- docs.cast.ai/docs/savings-report (fetched 2026-10-05): two savings sources; Realized (node-level) = projected − actual, card appears when node autoscaler manages ≥20% of nodes; Workload autoscaler savings card appears when ≥20% of workloads are in VPA mode; cards appear independently.
- docs.cast.ai/docs/savings-baseline (fetched 2026-10-05): WOOP savings formula `(original − current) × max(current, baseline price)`; WOOP accrues **independently of the baseline window** ("rightsizing isn't tied to when Cast AI took over node management"); baseline = fixed model frozen when CAST took over node management; Total savings = autoscaler savings only (WOOP already included via demand reduction — never double-count).
